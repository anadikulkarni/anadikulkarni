## About Me

I'm the primary data analyst at **Nilon's**, a national FMCG food company in India known for its pickles, spices, sauces, and confectionery. We're a two-person data team, a data engineer and me, so I work across the whole stack: Tableau dashboards for seven departments, machine learning models, generative AI tools, and the automation that ties them together.

I came to data through writing and research. As an academic editor I worked on 100+ economics and statistics manuscripts, and noticed that the most convincing papers rested on evidence rather than on assumptions about how people behave. To build a foundation that would enable me to be successful in this field, I applied to IIT Madras for a degree in programming and data science, which I began while still editing full-time.

What I enjoy most is the moment a chart makes obvious what a table of numbers had hidden. As AI writes more of the code, the analyst's job shifts from how to build something to what is worth building and why: asking the right question, then presenting the answer so people act on it.

## Intellectual Interests

### Literature

*Animal Farm* has been my favourite book since I read it at 12. It works as a simple story, but also invites you to dig deeper to uncover even greater insights. In a way, it perfectly represents the mission of the best data analysts. 

### The History of Ideas

I consider the history of ideas to be as important as political and military history, if not more so. Tracing the history of how humans see ourselves and our world goes a long way towards explaining how we behave as individuals and as a community. One of my favourite examples of this is Earthrise, a photograph from Apollo 8, the profound emotional impact it had on people, and how it changed how we saw the world and our beliefs about our place in the universe.

### The Classics

Between them, Herodotus and Thucydides describe my job: tell a story people remember, and maintain a rigorous dedication to the hard facts.

### Fantasy Fiction

My love for literature manifests best in my favourite genre: high fantasy. I love how exploring settings and themes far from modernity such as medieval times, magic, and other worlds manages to reveal truths about ourselves and our societies in the most interesting ways.
