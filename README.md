## About Me

I'm the primary data analyst at **Nilon's**, a national FMCG food company in India known for its pickles, spices, sauces, and confectionary. Our data team is two people, me and a data engineer, so I work across the whole stack: Tableau dashboards for seven departments, machine learning models, generative AI tools, and the automation that ties them together.

I came to data through writing and research. As an academic editor I worked on 100+ economics and statistics manuscripts, and noticed that the most convincing papers rested on evidence rather than on assumptions about how people behave. To build a foundation that would enable me to be successful in this field, i applied to IIT Madras for a degree in programming and data science, which I began while still editing full-time.

What I enjoy most is the moment a chart makes obvious what a table of numbers had hidden. As AI writes more of the code, I think the craft that makes a good data analyst of the future is multidisciplinary. Data analysts will spend less time writing code, and more time asking questions, and figuring out the best way to present the answers. Analysts will spend less time asking *how* to get something done, and more time asking *what* needs to be done, or even *why* it is an important pursuit.

## Intellectual Interests

- **History and the classics.** Between them, Herodotus and Thucydides describe my job: tell a story people remember, and get it right.
- ***Animal Farm*** is my favourite book since I read it age 12. A story that works on the literal layer, but also invites you to dig deeper to uncover even greater insights. In a way, it perfectly represents the mission of the best data analysts. 
- **The history of ideas.** I consider the history of ideas to be as important if not more than political and military history. Tracing the history of how humans see ourselves and our world goes a long way towards explaining how we behave as individuals and as a community. One of my favourite examples of this is Earthrise, a photograph from Apollo 8, the profound emotional impact it had on people, and how it changed how we saw the world and our beliefs about our place in the universe.
- **Fantasy worlds built in loving detail** My love for literature manifests best when I dig deep into my favourite genre: high fantasy. I love how digging deeper into things far from modernity, historical settings, magic, and other worlds manages to reveal truths about ourselves and our societies in the most interesting ways.
